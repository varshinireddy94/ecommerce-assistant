SHOPSPHERE ELECTRONICS WARRANTY POLICY

Eligible electronic products may include a manufacturer warranty or seller-provided warranty. The applicable warranty period depends on the individual product and is normally displayed on the product page or accompanying documentation.

A warranty generally covers manufacturing defects that occur during normal and intended use of the product.

A warranty claim may be considered when the product stops functioning correctly because of a manufacturing or material defect.

Warranty coverage generally does not include damage caused by:

- Accidental drops or impacts
- Liquid or moisture exposure
- Improper installation
- Unauthorized repairs or modifications
- Electrical surges or incompatible power sources
- Misuse or operation outside the manufacturer's instructions
- Normal cosmetic wear
- Scratches, dents, or other physical damage caused by the customer

The customer may be asked to provide the order ID, product serial number, photographs, videos, or a description of the problem.

ShopSphere or the authorized service provider may perform a diagnostic inspection before determining whether the issue is covered by warranty.

If the issue is covered, the available resolution may include repair, replacement, or another remedy specified by the manufacturer or seller.

A warranty claim does not automatically result in a refund.

If a product is still within its normal return period, the customer may have additional return or replacement options under the applicable return policy.

Once the standard return period has expired, eligible manufacturing defects may instead be handled through the warranty process.

Warranty coverage normally applies only to the product purchased through the applicable order and does not automatically extend to unrelated accessories or products.

The warranty period normally begins according to the manufacturer's or seller's stated warranty terms.

Customers should retain invoices, order information, warranty cards, and other purchase records when possible.

A warranty may become invalid if the product has been repaired or modified by an unauthorized service provider.

Warranty decisions are based on the applicable product warranty terms and the results of inspection or diagnosis.
