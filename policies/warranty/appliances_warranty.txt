SHOPSPHERE HOME APPLIANCE WARRANTY POLICY

Home appliances may carry a manufacturer or seller warranty covering eligible manufacturing defects.

The applicable warranty duration varies by product and is specified on the product listing, invoice, warranty documentation, or manufacturer terms.

Warranty coverage generally applies to defects that prevent the appliance from functioning as intended when the appliance has been used normally and according to the manufacturer's instructions.

Examples of potentially covered issues include:

- Manufacturing defects
- Component failures under normal use
- Electrical or mechanical failures covered by the manufacturer's warranty

Warranty coverage generally excludes problems caused by:

- Incorrect installation
- Improper electrical connections
- Accidental damage
- Water or moisture damage where not covered by the manufacturer
- Misuse or negligence
- Unauthorized modification or repair
- Normal wear and tear
- Damage caused by unsuitable operating conditions

For appliances requiring installation, the customer should follow the manufacturer's installation requirements.

A warranty claim may require the customer to provide the order ID, product details, serial number, photographs, or a description of the issue.

ShopSphere or an authorized service provider may arrange inspection or diagnosis.

If the defect is covered, the service provider may repair the appliance, replace eligible components, or replace the product according to the applicable warranty terms.

A warranty claim does not automatically qualify the customer for a refund.

If the product is still within its applicable return window, the customer may use the return or replacement process where permitted.

After the return window expires, manufacturing defects are generally handled through the warranty process.

Customers should retain proof of purchase and warranty documentation.

Unauthorized repairs or modifications may affect warranty eligibility.

The final warranty decision depends on the manufacturer's or seller's applicable warranty terms and the result of technical inspection.

Warranty coverage for accessories, installation services, consumables, and other related components may differ from coverage for the main appliance.
