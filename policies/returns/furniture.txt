SHOPSPHERE FURNITURE RETURN POLICY

Furniture and selected home furnishing products are generally eligible for return within 10 calendar days from the actual delivery date.

The product should normally be unused and undamaged and should include the components and accessories supplied with the original order.

Furniture that has been assembled, modified, painted, drilled, or otherwise altered after delivery may not qualify for a standard return.

If professional installation is required, customers should follow the applicable installation instructions.

If furniture arrives damaged, the customer should report the issue as soon as possible and preferably within 5 calendar days of delivery.

Photographs of the packaging and damaged areas may be requested during verification.

For large furniture items, ShopSphere may arrange inspection before approving a return.

Depending on the product and seller policy, the resolution may be a replacement, repair, or refund.

If only one component is damaged or missing, ShopSphere may provide the missing or replacement component instead of requiring the complete furniture item to be returned.

The return period begins on the actual delivery date.
