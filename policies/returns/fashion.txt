SHOPSPHERE FASHION RETURN POLICY

Fashion and lifestyle products are generally eligible for return within 10 calendar days from the actual delivery date.

Eligible resolutions may include a refund, replacement, or exchange depending on the product and seller.

The product should normally be unused, undamaged, and in resalable condition. Original packaging, tags, accessories, manuals, and promotional items should be retained whenever applicable.

Products showing signs of use, stains, scratches, damage, missing tags, or missing accessories may fail the return inspection.

If a fashion product arrives damaged, defective, or different from the product ordered, the customer should report the issue as soon as possible and preferably within 5 calendar days of delivery.

The customer may be asked to provide photographs or other information to verify the issue.

The return period is calculated from the actual delivery date, not the estimated delivery date.

If an order contains multiple products, return eligibility is evaluated separately for each item.
