SHOPSPHERE ELECTRONICS RETURN POLICY

Small electronics and electronic accessories are generally eligible for return within 7 calendar days from the actual delivery date.

Depending on the individual product, the available resolution may be replacement, refund, or authorized service support.

Electronic products should normally be returned with their original packaging, accessories, manuals, warranty documents, and other components supplied with the product.

The returned product must correspond to the product originally shipped. Serial numbers, IMEI numbers, and other identifying information may be checked during inspection.

If an electronic product arrives damaged, defective, or different from the product ordered, the customer should report the issue as soon as possible.

For damaged or defective electronics, ShopSphere may request photographs, videos, serial numbers, or other evidence.

Some electronics are replacement-only. If a replacement is available and the product-specific policy specifies replacement as the resolution, a refund may not be available initially.

If a manufacturing defect is discovered after the return period expires, the product may still qualify for manufacturer warranty service if it has an applicable warranty.

Physical damage caused by drops, liquid exposure, unauthorized repair, misuse, or accidents is generally not considered a manufacturing defect.
