SHOPSPHERE BEAUTY AND PERSONAL-CARE RETURN POLICY

Beauty and personal-care products may have restricted return eligibility because of hygiene and safety considerations.

Eligible unopened beauty products are generally returnable within 7 calendar days from the actual delivery date when the individual product listing allows returns.

Products that are opened, used, contaminated, or otherwise unsuitable for resale may not qualify for a standard return.

Personal-care and hygiene-sensitive products may be non-returnable even when other products in the same category are returnable.

If a beauty product arrives damaged, defective, expired, or different from the product ordered, the customer should report the issue promptly.

For damaged or incorrect products, ShopSphere may request photographs, batch information, packaging details, or other evidence.

A product specifically marked as non-returnable at the time of purchase is governed by that product-specific condition.

Defective or incorrectly delivered products may still be considered under the applicable exception process.
