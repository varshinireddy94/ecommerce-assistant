SHOPSPHERE GROCERY AND PERISHABLE PRODUCT RETURN POLICY

Fresh food, groceries, and other perishable products have a shorter reporting period because of their nature.

Customers should report damaged, spoiled, expired, or incorrect grocery products within 2 calendar days of delivery.

Perishable products are generally not eligible for ordinary returns based on customer preference.

If a grocery product is damaged, spoiled, expired, or materially different from what was ordered, ShopSphere may provide a refund or replacement after verification.

Customers may be asked to provide photographs showing the product condition, packaging, expiry information, or other relevant details.

Customers should retain the product and packaging until ShopSphere provides further instructions when verification is required.

Products that have been consumed, altered, or discarded before a claim is verified may be more difficult to investigate.

The applicable product listing may contain additional restrictions for certain food or perishable products.

The reporting period begins from the actual delivery date.
