SHOPSPHERE DELAYED ORDER POLICY

The estimated delivery date represents the expected delivery date of an order. It is not necessarily a guaranteed delivery date.

An order may arrive later than the estimated delivery date because of seller processing delays, transportation disruptions, weather, public holidays, logistics constraints, or other unforeseen circumstances.

If an order has passed its estimated delivery date, the customer should first check the latest order status and tracking information.

A delay beyond the estimated delivery date does not automatically mean that an order has been lost or cancelled.

ShopSphere may investigate delayed shipments with the seller or delivery partner.

Depending on the circumstances, a delayed order may:

- Continue toward delivery
- Receive a revised delivery estimate
- Be cancelled if it can no longer be fulfilled
- Be investigated as potentially lost

If ShopSphere or the seller cancels a prepaid order because the product cannot be delivered, the eligible payment amount will be refunded according to the refund policy.

Customers should not assume that a delayed order can automatically be cancelled simply because its estimated delivery date has passed.

If the order is still active and has already been shipped, cancellation may no longer be available through the normal cancellation process.

Customers should contact support if an order remains undelivered for an unusually long period or if tracking information indicates a serious delivery problem.

Severe weather, natural disasters, transportation disruptions, government restrictions, strikes, or other exceptional circumstances may cause delivery delays beyond the reasonable control of ShopSphere or its logistics partners.

During such events, estimated delivery dates may be revised.

If an order is confirmed as lost before delivery and cannot be fulfilled, the customer may become eligible for a replacement or refund depending on product availability and seller policy.
