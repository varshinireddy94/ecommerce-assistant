SHOPSPHERE STANDARD SHIPPING POLICY

ShopSphere provides an estimated delivery date when an order is placed. The estimated date represents the expected delivery time and is not necessarily a guaranteed delivery date.

After an order is placed, the seller may need to confirm inventory, process the order, and prepare the package for shipment.

Orders may move through the following stages:

Order placed
Order confirmed
Seller processing
Shipped
Out for delivery
Delivered
Cancelled

The exact status displayed to the customer depends on information received from the seller and delivery partner.

Actual delivery time can vary because of seller processing time, logistics availability, destination, weather, public holidays, transportation disruptions, and other unforeseen circumstances.

Customers should use the order ID to check the latest available order status.

Shipping charges may depend on product type, destination, seller, package size, delivery speed, and other applicable factors.

The applicable shipping charge should normally be displayed during checkout.

Some products may qualify for free shipping while others may carry a separate shipping fee.

Customers should verify their delivery address and contact information before placing an order.

An address can generally be changed only while the order remains at a stage where the seller or logistics partner can still modify the destination.

Once an order has progressed to shipment or dispatch, an address change may no longer be possible.

Tracking information may include shipment confirmation, dispatch information, estimated delivery date, out-for-delivery status, and delivery confirmation.

Tracking information may not update immediately after every physical movement of a package.

The actual delivery date recorded by the delivery partner is used when determining the beginning of a product's return period unless an investigation establishes that the delivery record is incorrect.
