SHOPSPHERE DELIVERY ISSUES POLICY

Customers should inspect packages when possible and report visible delivery problems promptly.

If a package has visible external damage, the customer should report the issue as soon as possible.

If the product inside the package is damaged, the customer should follow the damaged-product procedure under the applicable return policy.

The delivery of an apparently undamaged package does not prevent a customer from reporting a concealed product defect discovered after opening the package.

If tracking indicates that an order was delivered but the customer has not received it, the customer should contact ShopSphere support promptly.

ShopSphere may investigate delivery records with the seller or logistics partner.

If an order is confirmed as lost or incorrectly delivered, the customer may be eligible for a replacement or refund depending on product availability and seller policy.

If a package is delivered to an address different from the address associated with the order, the customer should contact ShopSphere rather than attempting to resolve the issue directly with another recipient.

ShopSphere may investigate the delivery location and coordinate with the delivery partner.

Delivery partners may make multiple delivery attempts depending on the destination and local delivery practices.

Customers should ensure that the delivery address and contact information provided during checkout are accurate.

Repeated failed delivery attempts may result in an order being returned to the seller.

If an order is returned because the customer was unavailable or provided an incorrect address, available refund or re-shipment options may depend on the seller's policy.

An order is normally considered delivered when the delivery partner records successful delivery to the customer or an authorized recipient.

The recorded delivery timestamp is normally used as the delivery date for return-window calculations unless an investigation establishes that the delivery record is incorrect.
