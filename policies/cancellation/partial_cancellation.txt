SHOPSPHERE PARTIAL ORDER CANCELLATION POLICY

An order containing multiple products may be eligible for partial cancellation if individual items have not progressed beyond the cancellation stage.

For example, if an order contains three products and only one product has already been shipped, the remaining unshipped products may potentially be cancelled.

Partial cancellation depends on whether the individual item can still be stopped before shipment.

If an individual item has already been shipped, that item generally cannot be cancelled through the normal cancellation process.

The customer may instead need to wait for delivery and use the applicable return procedure.

If an item is successfully cancelled before shipment and payment has already been collected, the eligible amount for that item will be refunded.

The refund amount may account for discounts, promotional offers, shipping charges, and other applicable adjustments.

Cancelling one item does not automatically cancel the remaining items in the order.

Each item may have a separate processing and shipment status.

Customers should provide the order ID and identify the specific product they want to cancel.

If multiple quantities of the same product were ordered, partial quantity cancellation may be possible depending on the order's current processing status.

If an item has already entered shipment processing, cancellation may no longer be available.

The latest item-level order information should be checked before confirming a partial cancellation.

If partial cancellation is unavailable, the customer may be able to return the relevant item after delivery according to the applicable return policy.
