SHOPSPHERE REFUND PROCESS POLICY

A refund is issued when an order or product qualifies for a refund under the applicable cancellation, return, damaged-product, delivery, or seller-failure policy.

A refund is different from a replacement or exchange. If a product is covered by a replacement-only policy and a valid replacement is available, the customer may not automatically receive a monetary refund.

A customer may become eligible for a refund when:

- An eligible order is cancelled before shipment
- A seller cancels a prepaid order
- An eligible returned product passes inspection
- A wrong or defective product cannot be replaced
- An order is confirmed lost or incorrectly delivered
- A product-specific policy explicitly provides a refund

For returned products, the refund is normally initiated after the returned product has been received and inspected.

The inspection may verify that the returned item matches the original order and satisfies the applicable return conditions.

If a return is rejected because the product does not meet the applicable return requirements, a refund may not be issued.

If only part of an order is cancelled or returned, the customer generally receives a partial refund corresponding to the eligible item and applicable charges.

For example, if an order contains three products and one eligible product is returned, the refund normally relates to the returned product rather than the entire order.

If a seller ships the wrong product or a product is confirmed defective or damaged during transit, ShopSphere may refund applicable shipping charges.

For customer-preference returns, shipping charges may not be refundable and return shipping costs may be deducted where applicable.

If an order is confirmed as lost before delivery and cannot be fulfilled, an eligible refund may be initiated.

Customers should not assume that an order is lost merely because tracking information has not changed for a short period. ShopSphere may investigate the shipment before approving the refund.

For wrong deliveries, ShopSphere may arrange a replacement or refund after verification.

Customers may be asked to provide photographs, packaging details, or other information to support the claim.

After a refund is approved, ShopSphere initiates the refund according to the applicable payment process.

The time required for the money to appear in the customer's account can depend on the payment method and financial institution.

A refund marked as initiated may still require additional processing by the customer's bank or payment provider.

Possible refund states include:

Refund not applicable
Refund pending approval
Refund approved
Refund initiated
Refund completed

Customers can use their order ID to request the current refund status.

ShopSphere may investigate suspicious or duplicate refund claims.

Submitting false information, returning a different product, or repeatedly making unsupported claims may result in refusal of the claim and restriction of future return privileges.

The final refund amount may depend on the product price, discounts, shipping charges, return deductions, partial refunds, and other applicable adjustments.
