SHOPSPHERE REFUND PAYMENT METHODS POLICY

Refunds are generally sent to the original payment method used for the transaction.

For prepaid card, bank, wallet, or other electronic payments, the refund may be processed through the corresponding payment channel.

The time required for a refund to appear in the customer's account depends partly on the payment provider and financial institution.

ShopSphere considers a refund processed once the refund transaction has been successfully initiated, even if the customer's bank takes additional time to reflect the amount.

For cash-on-delivery orders, the customer may be required to provide valid bank account or other eligible payment details before a refund can be processed.

The customer is responsible for providing accurate refund information.

ShopSphere is not responsible for delays caused by incorrect bank account or payment information supplied by the customer.

If the original payment method cannot accept the refund, ShopSphere may provide an alternative refund mechanism according to applicable procedures.

For eligible prepaid cancellations before shipment, the amount paid may be refunded through the original payment method.

If a seller cancels a prepaid order because the product is unavailable or cannot be fulfilled, the eligible amount paid by the customer will generally be refunded.

For cash-on-delivery orders cancelled before payment and delivery, no payment refund is normally required because payment was not collected.

If payment has already been collected and the order is subsequently cancelled or returned, the applicable refund procedure will apply.

Shipping charges may be treated differently depending on the reason for cancellation or return.

When the seller ships the wrong product or a product is confirmed defective or damaged during transit, applicable shipping charges may be refunded.

For customer-preference returns, shipping charges may not be refundable.

Customers should use their order ID when contacting ShopSphere regarding a refund.

The order ID allows the support system to identify the relevant order, payment status, refund amount, and refund processing state.
